## What is Confoederatio?

We're a group of researchers focused on a socioeconomic model of Earth from 10000BC to Now. As such, we work to supply both historic and real-time data at the most granular of levels for the entire globe.

Confoederatio also builds the software and mapping ecosystems needed to reconstruct, produce, and analyse vast quantities of human data.

All our data is accessible via [Dataview](https://dataview.confoederatio.org/). Here are just some of our projects:

- Borders: [Atlas](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Atlas) | Cities: [Stadestér](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Stadester) | Economics: [Eoscala](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/EoscalaVelkscala) | Population: [Velkscala](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/EoscalaVelkscala)
- Frameworks: [Collation](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Collation) | [Vercengen](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Vercengen) | ML: [Harbinger](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Harbinger)
- GIS: [Naissance](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Naissance)
- Software: [Dataview](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF/Dataview) | [Unitel OS](https://unitel.confoederatio.org/)

## How do I join?

1. Join either our [Discord](https://discord.gg/R9Q6xJb) or [Element](https://element.confoederatio.org).
2. Learn how to code. Learn [systems thinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_thinking).
3. Let us know [what you’re good at](https://forms.gle/RYfKbGJQLbJGY9kt7).

This repository also contains various Markdown files to help you and your agents get up to speed.
